Far Right Actors in Latin America a Classification

by Zoe Bosshard and Philipp Kerler

Note. This table contains a comprehensive list of far-right actors, i.e., actors who have been described as far right by scholars or political commentators. The list is as inclusive as possible, including debatable entries. Characteristics are classified according to the group theory of the far right, presented in the chapter "The Far Right in Latin America: Between Political Illiberalism and Economic Liberalism". Category 1 records whether the actor is characterized as illiberal and/or anti-pluralist (a negative assessment leads the group theory to reject the actor as far right, though the actor may still be illiberal/anti-pluralist without having been described as such). Category 2 collects characteristics defining in-groups and out-groups. Category 3 collects instrumental characteristics. The table is a perpetual work in progress, regularly maintained on the author's website. Credit for collecting most of the information presented in this table goes to Zoe Bosshard, many thanks!
